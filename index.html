Chapter 1 · Grade 2 · Light

The Shadow Thief

Every afternoon, Zara set up her puppet theatre in the garage. She pinned an old white sheet to the wall, switched on her torch and held up Rusty, her cardboard fox.

But something strange was happening. On Monday, Rusty’s shadow was as tall as the door. On Tuesday, it was smaller than Zara’s hand. On Wednesday, it vanished completely when her little brother Omar stood in the way.

“It’s a shadow thief,” Omar whispered. “It comes when the lights go off.”

Zara wasn’t so sure. She looked at the opaque cardboard fox, then at the bright light source in her hand. She had a feeling the answer was right in front of her…

Think like a scientist

Zara moves the torch closer to Rusty the fox. What do you predict will happen to the shadow?

In the full story, children test their prediction, record results in a table and find out who the “shadow thief” really is.
